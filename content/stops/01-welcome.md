**SAMPLE STOP — edit me via `/admin`.**

Welcome to this self-guided walk through Aztalan, a roughly 1,000-year-old
village site built by people of the Mississippian culture along the
Crawfish River. Early European-American settlers who found the earthworks
in the 1830s mistakenly linked them to the Aztec civilization and gave the
site its present name — the mounds and stockade you'll see are actually the
work of Woodland and Mississippian peoples who lived here long before.

As you walk the loop, look for the numbered posts. Each one matches a stop
in this guide. Tap a stop on the map or in the list to read more, see a
photo, or (once recorded) listen to the audio description.
